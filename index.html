    Hotel Agent

    From a guest’s question to a confirmed booking.

    AI AGENTS+

    A hotel front-desk and travel-shopping prototype with a guest widget, staff inbox, multilingual text, streaming English voice, and human handoff.

    Making actions recoverable

    • A recoverable quote → guest confirmation → submit → reconcile booking workflow, with idempotency and price-change reconfirmation.
    • Property-scoped retrieval with BM25, Arabic normalization, and optional embeddings.

    AdvancedRAG

    One retrieval framework. Interchangeable providers.

    GRADUATION PROJECT+

    My graduation project: a modular retrieval framework designed to keep the application independent of any single model provider or vector store.

    The engineering underneath

    • A provider factory for OpenAI, Cohere, and Google LLMs, with Qdrant or pgvector storage.
    • Token-aware chunking, semantic boundary detection, and hybrid vector, keyword, and metadata search.
    • Semantic caching, SQLAlchemy/Alembic migrations, and reproducible Docker deployment.

    Customer Support Platform

    The backend behind a support conversation.

    BACKEND+

    An asynchronous support backend managing customers, conversations, assignments, escalations, and call records through separate service and persistence layers.

    Keeping the workflow consistent

    • JWT authentication, role-based access, conversation ownership, and validated lifecycle transitions.
    • Assignment and escalation workflows, including a single unresolved escalation per conversation.
    • Provider-independent call metadata and tests for service, API, and database rules.

    Backend foundation. The frontend, LLM/RAG agents, and live telephony are planned extensions; call records do not place calls.
